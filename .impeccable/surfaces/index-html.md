# Surface: CV (cv.html)

Mode: Read. The visitor (recruiter, engineering manager) wants to understand fit fast: current role, multi-cloud operations depth, résumé, contact. Must read as a real CV, not a landing page. Must keep: project screenshots, Formspree contact form, automatic dark mode. Profile photo optional.

Memorable moment: the route strip — Klaten → Yogyakarta → Jakarta — with the current leg in service.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The CV is an itinerary of record: a dated, confirmed route of legs from Klaten to Jakarta, the current leg in service. It refuses the hero-plus-cards landing page and the sidebar résumé template.

OWN-WORLD: Cool ticket stock (#EEF1F4) and white printed panels; navy ink (#13202B); signal orange for the one live state only; route blue for the line. Barlow Condensed caps for names, cities and leg titles; Source Sans 3 reading face; JetBrains Mono only for dates, refs and codes. Dashed perforation rules between ticket parts, a rotated rubber stamp, round station markers on one continuous route line. No cards with coloured side borders, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: In one viewport the reader learns who, which role now, where the career travelled, and how to get the résumé. Scrolling, each leg reads as a calm itinerary entry; the page closes on a contact counter.

Below, a white route strip full width: three stations on a blue line, Jakarta struck in orange. Below that the start of the reading column: section index left, first leg (MEMBER.ID) with IN SERVICE stamp.
